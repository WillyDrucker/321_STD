When the user pushes back on a diagnosis ("that doesn't sound right", "this has to be a known issue"), stop and research the documented behaviour (the vendor's reference, its forums) before proposing a change. Prefer the cheapest read the system itself offers (one command, one sorted process list, one log line) over a capture file, a management console's timeline, or a visit, and never ask for a reboot or a trip when the system retries on its own.

**Why:** twice the first theory was wider than the evidence, and the user's challenge led to the documented cause: a single stale record in one case, a normal agent its owner recognized in the other. The user's experience with the system is evidence, and it has held each time.

**How to apply:** research first on a challenge, cite the source, say what is documented and what is inferred, and keep the fix to the one record or setting the evidence names. Related: [[feedback-verify-agent-claims]].
