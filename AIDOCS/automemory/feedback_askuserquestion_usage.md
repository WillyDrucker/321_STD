The AskUserQuestion form hides the reasoning behind the options, which makes it hard to answer. Use it only for straight questions on something already discussed. Anything that needs reasoning gets iterated in normal prose first - the user reads long-form.

**Why:** options without visible reasoning force a choice without the analysis that justifies it.

**How to apply:** discuss in prose, converge, then either act on the obvious answer or ask a short direct question. Previews and forms are for confirming, not for introducing, alternatives. See [[feedback-no-subagents-for-review]] for the same keep-it-with-the-lead spirit.
